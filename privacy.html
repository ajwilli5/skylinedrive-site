Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Skyline Drive, LLC website at skylinedrive.studio. Each Skyline Drive app has its own privacy policy, linked from that app's App Store listing.

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it has no forms or accounts. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information through this website.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically log technical information such as visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. That information is handled under GitHub's own privacy statement, and we do not receive or use it.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not share it.

Changes
